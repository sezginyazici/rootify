Rootify — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24.07.2026

This policy explains what Rootify ("the app", operated by Rootify) does with your data. Rootify simulates how a hair transplant result might look. It is a visualisation tool, not a medical service.

What we collect

Photos you take or choose. The photo you submit for a simulation, and the simulated images generated from it.

Purchase information. Whether you hold an active subscription, which plan, and when it renews.

App usage counts. How many free simulations you have run, so we can apply the free limit.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and we do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Where your photo goes

Rootify has no server of its own. Your photo is sent directly from your device to Replicate, Inc., which runs the AI models on our behalf.

Replicate passes the photo to the specific models we call: Google models (used to screen the photo and to generate the simulated images) and an OpenAI model (used to assess the scalp and produce the graft estimate).

Those providers process the photo only to return a result to us. We do not permit, and do not participate in, the use of your photo to train AI models.

Generated images are stored temporarily on Replicate's content delivery network so the app can display them. The links expire; we keep no copy on any server we control.

Because these providers operate internationally, your photo may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

What stays on your device

The photo you selected, the generated results while you are viewing them, and a small local record of how many free simulations you have used. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, Inc. on our behalf. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier for your installation, your subscription status, and a count of the free simulations you have run.

Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Why we process this data

To produce the simulation you asked for.

To check that a photo is usable before spending processing on it.

To apply the free-simulation limit and to unlock paid features.

Where the law requires a legal basis, ours is performance of a contract with you, together with your consent for the processing of your photo.

What we do not do

We do not track you across other apps or websites.

We do not show advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data.

We do not build a profile of you or use your photo for anything other than producing your simulation.

Your rights

You can delete everything the app holds by deleting the app from your device.

To ask what data is associated with your purchases, or to have it deleted, write to sezginyazici@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to processing, to request a copy of your data, or to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Rootify is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly process data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete any associated records.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and show the new version in the app before you next run a simulation.
