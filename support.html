2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

I encountered a bug in the app, what should I do?

Please send your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot or description of the bug to our support email.

How can I turn off Camera / Face Data permissions?

You can turn off Camera and related permissions at any time by going to your device's Settings > Rootify.

How can I delete my data or account?

You can delete your account from the in-app settings menu. Alternatively, you can send an email to sezginyazici@gmail.com with the subject "Account/Data Deletion Request" to request the permanent deletion of all your data from our systems.
